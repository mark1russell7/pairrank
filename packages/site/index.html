Which is better: one, or two?

pairrank shows you two sites. You pick the better one. After a few dozen picks, the ratings put all your sites in order.

The duel

or

Click a site to pick it. In the duel, the keys work too: ← and → pick a side, ↓ is a tie, and Backspace is an undo.

The ranking

110015001900

    The dot is the rating. The line is the rating plus and minus its uncertainty. Each site starts at 1500.

    A hidden taste

    The demo has a hidden taste: a fixed order of the eight sample sites. Let it pick, and see the ranking find its order.

    How the duel chooses a pair

    Each pair has an expected information. Two sites with near ratings give a result that is not sure. A site with few picks has a rating that is not sure. The duel shows the pair with the most information, so each pick teaches the ranking more.

    How the ratings work

    A pick is a win, a loss or a tie. The Bradley-Terry model finds the ratings that best explain all the picks together. Elo and Glicko-2 change the ratings one pick at a time. On this scale, 400 points give odds of 10 to 1.

    The app

    The app runs on your computer, as a desktop app or in a browser. It imports your bookmarks, shows two sites side by side, and keeps each pick in a local database. Each tag of your sites gets its own ranking.

    This demo runs the code of the app in your browser, with sample sites. Nothing leaves the page, and a reload starts it again.
